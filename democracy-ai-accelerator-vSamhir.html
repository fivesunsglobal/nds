A few practical questions

Before you apply

Is this right for me?

The accelerator is for AI-curious civic and nonprofit professionals, including executive directors, program managers and officers, communicators, researchers, fundraisers, and journalists. No coding or technical background is required. Bring a willingness to experiment and questions from your work.

What will the live sessions be like?

Each session combines short explanations, live demonstrations, guided experimentation, and discussion. You will work toward practical takeaways you can adapt to your role. The final session includes a guest expert and your AI Action Showcase.

How much time should I set aside?

Five live sessions of 90 minutes, on November 5, 12, and 19 and December 3 and 10, 2026, from 2:00–3:30 PM Eastern. There is no live session during Thanksgiving week. Practice Sprints between sessions are short and optional. You can also sign up for individual coaching.

How does one-on-one coaching work?

There will be two opportunities for participants to sign up for coaching. Bring a specific challenge, an experiment, or a question. Booking details will be shared with the cohort.

Do I need paid AI tools?

Some demonstrations and automation features may require a paid subscription or particular tool access. We will share preparation guidance before the cohort. Tell us in your application if your organization restricts which tools you can use so we can help assess fit.

What about privacy and sensitive information?

Responsible use is part of the learning throughout. We will discuss verification, bias, privacy, organizational policies, and when human judgment must lead. Use public, sample, or appropriately anonymized material for exercises, and follow your organization’s rules.

Is there a capstone or homework?

There is no formal capstone project. Optional Practice Sprints help you try something useful between sessions. For the final AI Action Showcase, prepare a two-minute idea: the problem or context, your proposed action, and the impact you hope to make. No slides are required.

How do I earn the certificate?

Complete the attendance requirement, share an idea in the final session or submit a brief written version, and complete the final feedback survey. The specific attendance requirement will be confirmed before enrollment.

What if the dates or language do not work for me?

Join the future-cohort interest list and tell us your time zone and preferred language. Spanish-language cohorts are planned. We will also share upcoming Salons and other opportunities. This cohort is taught in English.

What happens after I apply?

We review applications for fit and available places. If accepted, you will receive enrollment information and a payment link. Applying does not involve payment or guarantee a place. The planned payment deadline is October 30, 2026. Program terms, including cancellation and refund details, will be shared before payment.
