Funding pipeline

27 opportunities and $33M identified in under two hours

A regional representative · Global nonprofit

12× faster donor research$5.75M FY26 pipeline target
Read the case study

Context

Finding foundation, bilateral, and other non-U.S. government funding required serial manual searches that could consume two working days—time urgently needed for strategy and relationship-building.

Action

The participant used enterprise-approved AI tools with voice and chat to refine the research approach, then batch-researched donor profiles using live web sources and approved internal documents.

Impact

They identified 27 opportunities totaling $33M in under two hours. Donor research fell from roughly 1.5 days to under 45 minutes, returning time to prioritization, relationships, and delivery.

Responsible AI in practice

  • Verified every output against primary sources.
  • Excluded outdated information and shared no confidential partner data.
  • Kept strategic framing and final prioritization in human hands.
Proposal research

Four days of proposal research compressed into one

A program professional · Global nonprofit

4× faster research100 international leaders
Read the case study

Context

A time-sensitive leadership-exchange proposal required country research, city-level workforce and supply-chain analysis, and evidence-based pairings across four countries.

Action

The participant used enterprise-approved AI research tools to refine selection criteria, evaluate U.S. cities, propose city–country pairings, and draft a background statement aligned with the funder’s priorities.

Impact

About four days of research became one—a 75% reduction in effort—while producing a fuller evidence base for a proposal designed to reach 100 leaders across education, industry, and government.

Responsible AI in practice

  • Prompted for geographic and demographic diversity to counter familiar-place bias.
  • Cross-checked research with government and institutional sources.
  • Reviewed every recommendation before using it in the proposal.
Decision-support agent

An AI guide that helps every program articulate value

A technical specialist · Global nonprofit

5 methods synthesized1 scalable agent
Read the case study

Context

Teams needed a shared way to reason about cost-effectiveness and value, especially where civic participation and leadership outcomes resist simple financial measurement.

Action

The participant combined deep research, staff interviews, and internal resources to compare five ROI-adjacent methods, build a practical guide, and turn it into a decision-support agent on an enterprise-approved AI platform.

Impact

Teams can now explore an approach suited to their context and communicate value with data they already have. The guidance is self-service, consistent, and scalable across programs.

Responsible AI in practice

  • Hard-coded the agent not to calculate ROI ratios, infer economic value, or rank programs.
  • Uses only approved frameworks and discloses that it is AI-powered.
  • Requires human review and provides a route to a human expert.

A few practical questions

Before you apply

Is this right for me?

The accelerator is for AI-curious civic and nonprofit professionals, including executive directors, program managers and officers, communicators, researchers, fundraisers, and journalists. No coding or technical background is required. Bring a willingness to experiment and questions from your work.

What will the live sessions be like?

Each session combines short explanations, live demonstrations, guided experimentation, and discussion. You will work toward practical takeaways you can adapt to your role. The final session includes a guest expert and your AI Action Showcase.

How much time should I set aside?

Between the 90-minute live sessions and optional practice sprints (homework) and coaching, plan for about two hours per week — more if you spend more time with the material and community. For those celebrating holidays in late November, we'll skip the last week of November.

What do I need to participate technically?

Join from a quiet place with reliable Wi-Fi and a computer with a working microphone and speakers or headphones. Live sessions take place on Google Meet, so you will need an up-to-date web browser that supports it. We will share the meeting link and any preparation guidance before the cohort begins.

How does one-on-one coaching work?

There will be two opportunities for participants to sign up for coaching. Bring a specific challenge, an experiment, or a question. Booking details will be shared with the cohort.

Do I need paid AI tools or new accounts?

No one will be required to pay for a tool or create an account they are not comfortable using. You can still follow the demonstrations and participate in discussions without doing so. Your experience will be richer if you are logged in to AI tools you already use—or feel comfortable trying—so you can experiment alongside the facilitators. Some advanced features may depend on your subscription or organizational access. Tell us in your application if your organization restricts which tools you can use so we can help assess fit.

Is the accelerator tied to one AI tool?

No. The accelerator is tool-agnostic. Your facilitators have deep experience teaching major LLM tools, including ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot, and focus on transferable skills you can use across platforms: prompting, verification, responsible use, and workflow design.

What about privacy and sensitive information?

Responsible use is part of the learning throughout. We will discuss verification, bias, privacy, organizational policies, and when human judgment must lead. Use public, sample, or appropriately anonymized material for exercises, and follow your organization’s rules.

Is there a capstone or homework?

There is no formal capstone project. Optional Practice Sprints help you try something useful between sessions. For the final AI Action Showcase, prepare a two-minute idea: the problem or context, your proposed action, and the impact you hope to make. No slides are required.

How do I earn the certificate?

Complete the attendance requirement, share an idea in the final session or submit a brief written version, and complete the final feedback survey. The specific attendance requirement will be confirmed before enrollment.

What if the dates or language do not work for me?

Join the future-cohort interest list and tell us your time zone and preferred language. Spanish-language cohorts are planned. We will also share upcoming Salons and other opportunities. This cohort is taught in English.

What happens after I apply?

We review applications for fit and available places on a rolling basis. If accepted, you will receive enrollment information and a payment link. Applying does not involve payment or guarantee a place. The planned payment deadline is October 30, 2026.
