Why Democracy+AI

Civic organizations are being asked to do more while losing room to act

Across the world, civic space is narrowing. Funding and cross-border collaboration are being restricted. Organizations are reducing staff or closing altogether, while credible voices are silenced or shut out of consequential debates.

At the same time, AI is changing how quickly organizations can research, communicate, organize, and learn. Those capabilities are already reshaping political power and the information environment.

AI cannot replace trusted relationships, local knowledge, courage, or judgment. It can help people with those qualities extend their reach—giving smaller teams more capacity and creating space for work that only humans can do.

The opportunity

Put powerful tools in the hands of people working toward a more democratic future.

FAQ

What to expect

Do I need previous AI experience?

No. The cohort is designed for AI-curious professionals who want a practical foundation. You should be comfortable using everyday web applications, but no coding or technical background is required.

Is this primarily lectures?

No. Each session combines concise explanations, live demonstrations, guided experimentation, discussion, and a practical takeaway you can continue using.

Who is the cohort for?

It is designed for executive directors, program managers, program officers, and other civic professionals responsible for programs, strategy, communications, fundraising, or organizational operations.

What if my organization handles sensitive information?

Privacy, verification, information risk, and responsible adoption are integrated throughout. The cohort will help you distinguish appropriate experiments from tasks that require stronger safeguards or should not use public AI tools.

Will sessions be recorded?

This remains to be confirmed before registration opens. The cohort is designed around live participation, and any recording policy will account for participant privacy.

Are scholarships available?

A limited number of reduced-price places will be available by request. We will include a simple request option in the final registration process.
