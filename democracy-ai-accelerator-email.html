Democracy is in decline globally, and civic organizations are under attack and losing funding while authoritarian actors use AI to control the information environment, which increasingly rewards manipulation.

Civil society has important advantages—community connection, trusted relationships, and shared values. But if we can't reach people and connect them, we have no influence. Any civic professional can pick up AI tools today and start to save time, build influence, and amplify impact in a responsible way.

AI cannot replace trusted relationships, local knowledge, courage, or judgment. It can help people with those qualities—and their ideas—travel farther. The Democracy+AI Accelerator is designed to get you there.

Democracy+AI is a six-week live learning experience for non-technical democracy and civil society professionals (self-assessed) who have heard about AI tools and maybe tested a few, and who are looking for practical, responsible ways to use AI in their work.
