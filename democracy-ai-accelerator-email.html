EARLY-BIRD PRICING THROUGH OCT 16 · APPLICATIONS DUE OCT 26

Democracy is in decline globally, and civic organizations are under attack and losing funding. Authoritarian actors are using AI at scale to control the information environment, which increasingly rewards manipulation.

Civil society has important advantages—community connection, trusted relationships, and shared values. But if we cannot reach people and connect them, we have no influence. AI is not the only solution, but it is something any organization and civic professional can pick up now to save time, build influence, and amplify impact.

AI cannot replace trusted relationships, local knowledge, courage, or judgment. It can help people with those qualities—and their ideas—travel farther. Democracy+AI is designed to get you there.

The Democracy+AI Accelerator is a six-week live learning experience for non-technical democracy and civil society professionals (self-assessed) who have heard about AI tools and maybe tested a few, and who are looking for practical, responsible ways to use AI in their work.
