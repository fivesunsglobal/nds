Why now

Democracy needs new ways to compete, connect, and deliver

The traditions of democracy still matter. But the institutions and practices built around them have not kept pace with a world transformed by technology, fractured information, rising authoritarian power, and declining public trust.

Civic organizations make democratic power real

They help people organize, speak, solve problems, build communities, and hold institutions accountable. Yet they are being asked to do more with fewer resources while funding tightens, cross-border collaboration is restricted, and the information environment grows faster and more sophisticated.

Supporting civic organizations is central to the Studio’s work—but the larger purpose is to help democracy evolve: carrying forward what remains essential while developing approaches equal to the present and the future.

We need a new way of doing democracy—one that can protect democratic space, lower barriers to participation, and help credible voices reach and move people.

Ideas & Convening

Generating and promoting new approaches to democracy in the age of AI

New Democracy Studio creates space for critical perspectives that can change how democratic problems are understood—and what people do next.

We develop and publish analysis at the intersection of democracy, technology, and the information environment, bring people together around the questions it raises, and look for ideas that can be tested in practice.

Experts, academics, public officials, private-sector leaders, and civic actors from around the world help us challenge assumptions, compare experience across sectors and borders, and see possibilities that would be difficult to find alone.

Convening is how we generate and road-test ideas. The strongest can become programs, partnerships, or experiments tested in context.
