Why now

Democracy needs institutions that can keep pace with change

Civic organizations—nonprofits, advocates, independent media, researchers, community leaders, and movements—make democratic participation real. Their work is more critical than ever, but the environment around them is becoming faster, noisier, and more technologically sophisticated.

New Democracy Studio strengthens the people and organizations doing this work while creating space to examine a larger question: what must democracy become to remain relevant, responsive, and capable in the present and the future?

Why a studio

Ideas move through conversation into practice

We develop propositions, challenge them with people who see the world differently, turn the strongest into programs, and learn from what happens in context.

Ideas

Critical thinking for difficult democratic problems

A small internal think tank with an experimental instinct.

We publish unconventional analysis and develop ideas at the intersection of democracy, technology, and the information environment. The goal is not commentary for its own sake, but thinking that can gather people, change the conversation, and ultimately be tested in practice.

Convening

Putting important ideas in contact with real experience

The Studio hosts focused conversations with experts, academics, public officials, private-sector leaders, civic actors, and thinkers from around the world.

Convening is how we generate, challenge, and road-test ideas. Our power comes from a global network of allies and our ability to elevate perspectives that are too often separated by geography, sector, or professional language.

01Private working conversations

Small groups assembled to pressure-test an emerging proposition.

02Public dialogues

Accessible conversations about where democracy is heading and what the future might require.

03Cross-sector exchange

Civic, public, academic, and private-sector experience brought into the same room.

About

Ideas informed by practice—and practice sharpened by ideas

Adam Fivenson

Chief Democracy Officer

New Democracy Studio builds on Adam’s more than two decades leading international development programs and helping civic organizations use technology thoughtfully and effectively.

His work has included technology training, human-centered and community-driven design in countries including Guatemala and Afghanistan, and think-tank work within a major global democracy-focused foundation. The Studio brings that practical program experience into close contact with ideas, discourse, and debate—then directs the resulting creativity toward problems that matter for a democratic future.
