Ideas

Put overlooked perspectives into play

Some democratic problems require more than familiar answers.

We develop and publish clear, sometimes unorthodox analysis at the intersection of democracy, technology, and the information environment—then bring those ideas into conversation with people who can challenge, improve, and act on them.

Convening

Get the right people in the room

Focused conversations connect civic leaders with experts, academics, public officials, private-sector leaders, and thinkers from around the world.

We use private working sessions, public dialogues, and cross-sector exchange to surface perspectives that rarely meet, pressure-test emerging approaches, and find the ideas worth taking further.

The goal is not simply a good conversation. It is a better decision, a stronger idea, or the beginning of something useful.

Why a studio

Built to try what democracy needs next

We bring people together, test promising ideas in context, and turn what works into programs that can travel. Our global network gives the Studio its reach; practical experience keeps the work grounded.

About

Ideas informed by practice—and practice sharpened by ideas

Adam Fivenson

Chief Democracy Officer

New Democracy Studio builds on Adam’s more than two decades leading international development programs and helping civic organizations use technology thoughtfully and effectively.

His work has included technology training, human-centered and community-driven design in countries including Guatemala and Afghanistan, and think-tank work within a major global democracy-focused foundation. The Studio brings that practical program experience into close contact with ideas, discourse, and debate—then directs the resulting creativity toward problems that matter for a democratic future.
