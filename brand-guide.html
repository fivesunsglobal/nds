01 · Identity

Logo system

The full wordmark is the primary signature. Use the light version on white or very light backgrounds and the reversed version on New Democracy Studio Navy. Use the compact NDS mark when space is limited.

Clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter N in NEW. Use the full logo at no less than 180 px wide digitally or 48 mm in print. Use the NDS mark at no less than 48 px digitally or 13 mm in print.

03 · Editorial hierarchy

Make every level unmistakable

Use one consistent, left-aligned entry pattern for every top-level website section. Place the section header before columns, cards, or other content so the hierarchy is clear before the layout changes.

What we do

We help civic organizations become more capable, influential, and resilient—so democracy can be too

New Democracy Studio develops practical programs that help civic actors build capacity, strengthen their influence, and navigate a rapidly changing technological and information environment.

Why now

Subsection headings are smaller, use sentence case, and do not repeat the blue uppercase section label treatment.

Page heroReserve the largest type and unique navy treatment for the site’s primary proposition.
Section labelUse small blue uppercase text that matches the corresponding navigation item.
Section headlineUse a large left-aligned serif heading, followed by optional smaller supporting copy.
Subsection headingUse a medium serif heading in sentence case. Do not use the uppercase section-label style.
Layout orderComplete the full-width section header before beginning columns, cards, grids, or forms.
Section boundariesUse background changes and the yellow top rule to distinguish top-level sections.
Image collectionsWhen several stories carry equal weight, use a complete grid rather than a carousel. Keep imagery dominant and place only location and title in a high-contrast overlay.

04 · Application

Use the identity consistently

DoUse approved artwork, preserve proportions, maintain clear space, and choose the version with strong contrast.
Do notStretch, crop, rotate, recolor, outline, add shadows, or place the logo on a visually busy background.
Color rolesNavy provides authority and depth. Blue signals technology and connection. Yellow marks action, progress, and emphasis.
VoicePractical, human-centered, thoughtful, confident, globally aware, and free of unnecessary technical jargon.

Digital patterns

Use rounded buttons for actions, generous white space, crisp yellow section rules, restrained editorial reveals, and navy panels for high-priority information.
