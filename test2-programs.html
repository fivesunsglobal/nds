DEMOCRACY+AI

Help democratic people and institutions lead in the age of AI

Democracy+AI combines a free monthly Salon, an applied accelerator cohort, organizational training, longer implementation support, and partner-sponsored cohorts. It is the primary way for individuals and institutions to engage with the Studio.
