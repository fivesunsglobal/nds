Last updated: August 21, 2026
New Democracy Studio is a project of Five Suns Global, LLC. Five Suns Global, LLC is the legal operator of this website and the controller of personal information collected directly through New Democracy Studio.

What we collect

We may collect information you choose to provide when you request a seat in a program, contact us, join a mailing list, or otherwise interact with New Democracy Studio. This may include your name, email address, organization, role, country, program interests, and information you include in free-text responses.

How we use information

We use personal information to respond to inquiries, review and administer program requests, communicate with participants, improve programs, and maintain relationships with organizations and partners. We send broader updates about future programs only when you have asked to receive them or when another lawful basis applies.

Legal bases for processing

Where applicable under data-protection law, we process information because it is necessary to respond to your request or take steps related to a potential program or working relationship; because we have a legitimate interest in operating and improving New Democracy Studio; or because you have given consent, such as for optional program updates.

Service providers and third parties

We may use service providers to operate programs and communications, including email, form, scheduling, videoconferencing, and website-hosting services. Information submitted through forms on this site is processed by Formspree under its applicable terms and privacy practices.

This site is designed to operate without advertising pixels or unnecessary analytics. The website may be hosted using GitHub Pages or another hosting provider, which may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs for security and service operation.

Data retention

We retain personal information while it remains relevant to the program, inquiry, working relationship, or purpose for which it was collected, and for any additional period reasonably necessary for legal, administrative, or recordkeeping obligations. Mailing-list information is retained until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, subject to applicable legal requirements.

International transfers

New Democracy Studio serves an international audience and uses service providers that may process information in the United States or other countries. Where required, we take reasonable steps to use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or portability of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical steps to protect personal information. No online system can guarantee absolute security, so please avoid sending highly sensitive, confidential, or at-risk information through public website forms or ordinary email.

Children

New Democracy Studio programs and this website are intended for professional audiences and are not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our programs, technology, or legal obligations change. The date above shows when it was last revised.
